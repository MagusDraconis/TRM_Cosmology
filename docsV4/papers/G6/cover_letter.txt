We submit the manuscript entitled "Bilocal Gravity: 1-Loop Finiteness, Unitarity,

SUMMARY OF RESULTS
──────────────────
This paper reports a mechanism for rendering perturbative quantum gravity finite
at the 1-loop level, using a bilocal form factor that suppresses the graviton
propagator at high momentum. The key results are:

1. Explicit 1-loop graviton self-energy computation. The bubble diagram is
   evaluated numerically and found to be UV-finite. No counterterms are required
   at 1-loop, in contrast to GR where four counterterms (R, R^2, R_{munu}^2,
   Riem^2) are needed.

2. 1-loop unitarity. The optical theorem gives Im Pi >= 0, and the
   Kallen-Lehmann spectral density satisfies rho(s) >= 0. All five perturbative
   unitarity criteria are met at the 1-loop level.

3. 2-loop power counting. The superficial degree of divergence D = -26 for the
   sunset diagram (GR: D = +6). The Goroff-Sagnotti R^3 obstruction of GR is
   absent. A conditional all-orders convergence theorem is formulated.

4. The fundamental bilocal field K(x,y) is shown to be gauge-invariant, so the
   theory does not require a Faddeev-Popov ghost sector at the fundamental level.
   Gauge symmetry and ghosts appear only in the derived metric-perturbation
   description.

LIMITATIONS (EXPLICITLY STATED IN THE PAPER)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
- The continuum limit of the underlying discrete lattice theory is not proven
  (a standard constructive QFT challenge shared by all interacting theories in 4D).
- The 2-loop integral has not been numerically evaluated; the Goroff-Sagnotti
  absence is inferred from power counting.
- The all-orders theorem is conditional on three explicit assumptions, each
  carrying strong evidence but not yet formally proven without conditionality.

The paper does not claim to solve quantum gravity. It reports a concrete
mechanism---the bilocal form factor---with explicit 1-loop numerical verification
and well-defined limitations.
