## Abstract (Draft)

The Temporal Rate Matrix / Temporal Quantum Matrix (TRM/TQM) framework (V3.4, frozen) describes collective frequency emergence in finite coupled phase-oscillator lattices as a constrained effective theory with exactly two irreducible structural inputs: the closure-family ansatz (I1) and the bridge-band prior (I2). The oscillator dynamics are fully characterized — the formal proof scaffold FP01–FP31 is closed, the bridge band is classified as an imposed structural input (CLASS D), and the rational ladder is an induced consistency structure, not a dynamical quantization effect.

V4 introduces a pure **interpretation layer** on top of the frozen core. Without modifying any oscillator equations, assumptions, or proof results, we explore the physical meaning of the emergent collective frequency Ω* and the clock-bias parameter φ through a time-rate field interpretation:

```
T(x) := Ω*(x)           (local time-rate field)
φ(x) := T(x) − 1        (spatial field input)
a(x) := c² · ∇T(x)      (effective gravitational acceleration)
```

We test candidate physical mappings for φ(x) against three mandatory consistency checks derived from the V3.4 core: (1) E1 compatibility — Ω* must remain globally constant, without q-dependence; (2) I1 compatibility — rational structure Ω = 1 + m/q must persist; (3) I2 compatibility — φ(x) must remain compatible with the bridge band [0.16, 0.19] for the target system.

The classical gravity candidate φ = −GM/(c²r) passes checks 1 and 2 but fails check 3 at galactic scales (φ ~ 5×10⁻⁶ vs. required φ ~ 0.17, a factor of 34,000×). This candidate is formally valid only in the compact-object regime (r ~ 3r_s). Alternative candidates — exponential decay, constant-plus-perturbation, and medium/buoyancy interpretation — are outlined for future evaluation.

V4 does not claim to derive gravity from first principles, to replace General Relativity, or to resolve the scale mismatch. It provides a structured framework for exploring the physical content of the TRM/TQM core without compromising the frozen theory's integrity.
