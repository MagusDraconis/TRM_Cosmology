## 2. Methodology

**Counterfactual trace:** Match profiles with similar baseline states but different
c3OmgS outcomes. Identify which variables separate outcome groups. Variables that
do NOT differ between matched groups cannot be causal drivers.

**Natural variation:** Use naturally occurring profile-to-profile variance (no
perturbation) to test whether diagnostic relationships hold under stratification.
Relationships that fail under stratification are not causally robust.
