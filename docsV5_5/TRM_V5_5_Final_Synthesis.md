## Central Finding

**The RecoverFP branch geometry is generated by a distributed multi-stage d-to-K amplification mechanism requiring all 5 epochs of the full update cycle.**

### UME: No single stage dominates

All D/E stages amplify distance and coupling metrics 2-14x from Epoch 1 to Epoch 5. Omega remains nearly constant. No individual stage produces the majority of branch separation.

### UMA: Synchronized d-to-K amplification

Distance and coupling separation curves are nearly perfectly synchronized (rho approximately 0.99-1.00). Neither d-to-K nor K-to-d consistently leads. Omega is definitively downstream (max separation 0.80-1.14 vs d/K 1.86-3.31).

### UMI: Full map required

- Epoch 1 truncation: ALL 5 high-branch seeds eliminated.
- Epochs 2-4 truncation: Only 1/30 high-branch seeds emerge.
- Epoch 5: Full recovery of all 5 high-branch seeds.
- Freeze-K at ANY epoch: 4-5 of 5 high-branch seeds eliminated.

**Early commitment is FALSIFIED. The d-to-K update loop is NECESSARY. Full 5-epoch map is required.**
