## 2. Research Question

**What generates rawIQR variation between profiles before SelectAndClassify?**

V5.53 established that rawIQR is the dominant P1/P1b discriminator (~10× normalized dominance). But V5.53 did not identify *where* rawIQR variation comes from. RIO_01 asks: is rawIQR variation generated by seed realization, the random frequency generator, topology/graph structure, profile construction, or something hidden?

## 5. Decision Model

### Model A: Seed Realization

**rawIQR is a pure function of seed-determined weight sampling + N.**

The generator (uniform[0.90, 1.10]) sets the expected IQR = S = 0.10. The seed determines which specific realization is drawn. Between-seed variance dominates (~10×). Topology and graph structure are independent (rawIQR computed before coupling). High-rawIQR profiles differ from low-rawIQR only in spread — not in central tendency, bounds, or N.

This means: **rawIQR variation is sampling noise.** Two profiles with the same N and different seeds will have different rawIQR purely because they drew different random weights from the same uniform distribution.
