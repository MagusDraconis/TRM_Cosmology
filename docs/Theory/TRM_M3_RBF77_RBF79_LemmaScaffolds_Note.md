## 1) RBF77: Topological Necessity Lemma Scaffold

- **Statement:** Selection of `m=3` becomes topologically necessary if the phase closure defect `|qΩ - p|` is uniquely minimized by `m=3` inside the structurally derived `qCore` domain.
- **Prerequisites:** Rational phase lattice assumption, integer winding cycle constraint, structurally derived bridge `qCore` domain.
- **Counterexample Classes:** Alternative `m`-modes satisfying the defect bound within `qCore` without incurring heavier action penalties.
- **Open Proof Step:** Analytically derive the uniqueness of the phase defect minimum for `m=3` across the bounded `qCore` space.

## 2) RBF78: Energy-Margin Sufficiency Lemma Scaffold

- **Statement:** The constraints are mathematically sufficient for `m=3` selection if the shared energy margin `ΔE > 0` against all admissible competitors.
- **Prerequisites:** Formulation of the shared minimal functional, bounded action-stationarity residual limiting admissibility.
- **Counterexample Classes:** Admissible competitors achieving a lower or equal shared functional energy (`ΔE <= 0`).
- **Open Proof Step:** Prove analytically that `ΔE > 0` is universally guaranteed across the defined admissible domain.

---

## 3) RBF79: Domain-Closure Boundary Lemma Scaffold

- **Statement:** The validity domain for the `m=3` selection rule is strictly bounded by analytical limits `ε_phase` and `ε_action`, outside which the formal rule must gracefully abstain.
- **Prerequisites:** Continuous parameterization of operational selection limits, explicit classification map of boundary failure regimes.
- **Counterexample Classes:** Parameter regimes where the formal selection rule generates confident false-positive selections outside the physically valid domain.
- **Open Proof Step:** Derive absolute asymptotic limits for `ε_phase` and `ε_action` directly from lattice-energy normalization principles.
