## A. Executive Summary

The Temporal Rate Matrix / Temporal Quantum Matrix (TRM/TQM) project investigates collective
frequency emergence in finite coupled phase-oscillator lattices and its consequences for
internal geometry, synchronization structure, and effective observables. The framework is
classified as a **constrained effective theory** — not zero-parameter, not purely
phenomenological, but fully closed under two irreducible structural inputs (I1: closure-family
ansatz; I2: bridge-band prior).

The project follows a strict **test-first methodology** with claim discipline enforced at
every phase. No theorem-level claims are made where only numerical evidence exists. No physical
constants are claimed as derived. The governing mantra is: *candidate only, not theorem-level,
not first-principles closure.*

The core research workflow is:

```
Protocol → Execution → Freeze → Audit → Comparison → Interpretation → Synthesis
```

Each version phase defines a protocol (forbidden actions, anti-feedback gates, permission
scopes), executes predictions or analyses under that protocol, freezes results with SHA-256
hashing, audits for tampering or hidden tuning, compares results against immutable references,
interprets findings under strict claim governance, and synthesizes a branch completion report.
This workflow was formalized in V4.5 and has governed every subsequent phase.

## B. Origins

### Clockwork Cosmology V1

The earliest formulation introduced a **variable local time-rate factor** as the driver of
gravitational-like behavior in a flat-space coordinate picture. Key concepts included:

- **Time-aether concept:** a background temporal medium whose local rate varies with
  gravitational potential
- **Temporal gradients:** spatial variations in the local time rate drive effective
  acceleration
- **Gravity interpretation:** gravity is not a force transmitted through space but a
  consequence of spatially varying time-flow rates
- **Cosmological interpretation:** a global time-rate gradient across cosmological scales

**Historical origin only.** The V1 formulation is a conceptual hypothesis, not equivalent to
the current claim set. The language evolved from narrative "Time-Aether" to the scalar field
formalism of TRM. No V1 claims are carried forward without independent re-verification under
current governance.

**This is the most important V5.2 finding.**

**Seed stability** and **regime stability** are distinct concepts:

- **Seed stability:** How much do results vary when changing only the random seed (fixed

- **Regime stability:** How much do results vary when changing regime parameters (e.g., ξ,
